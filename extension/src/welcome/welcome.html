Earmark is ready

One thing to try, and one decision to make. Both take a minute.

Try it now

  1. Open any article in another tab.
  2. Press Alt + P.

That is the whole thing. The sentence being read is highlighted on the page itself, and it follows along as it scrolls.

The rest of the keys

Alt+PPlay or pause
Alt+SRead just the selected text
Alt+← →Back or forward a sentence
Alt+clickStart reading from that paragraph

Choose how it sounds

Right now Earmark uses the voices your computer already has. They work instantly and need no download, and most of them are fairly robotic.

The natural voices are the reason this extension exists. They run on your own machine, so they are free and work offline, and nothing you read is sent anywhere. They need a one-time download of about 88 MB.

You can change this at any time in settings, and remove the download again from the same place.

Worth knowing

  • Nothing leaves your device unless you pick a voice labelled “needs a connection”, which are provided by your browser rather than by us.
  • It remembers where you stopped. Come back later and it resumes at the right sentence, even if the page has been edited.
  • PDFs work too. Open one and press the same key.
  • Continuing to the next page is off by default. Turn it on in settings if you want long articles read end to end.
