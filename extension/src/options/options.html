Highlighting

Colours apply to the text highlighted on the page as it is read. The defaults meet contrast requirements against both light and dark pages, and the spoken word is underlined as well as filled so the cue never depends on colour alone.

Pronunciation

Names and unusual words are where any reader gets caught out. Add a word here with how it should sound, spelled the way you would say it, and it is fixed everywhere from the next sentence on. Your entries override the built-in ones.
