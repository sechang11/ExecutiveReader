What it does that others do not

Natural voices, no subscription

Most readers give you robotic voices free and charge monthly for good ones, because the good ones run on someone else's servers. This runs a neural model on your machine instead.

Highlights the real page

The sentence being read lights up in place, on the actual page, along with the word being spoken. Nothing is rewritten or re-laid-out, so sites keep working normally while you listen.

Remembers where you stopped

Come back a week later and it resumes at the right sentence, even if the page has been edited since. It matches on the text, not a position.

Keeps going

Follows the page as it scrolls, notices when more content loads, and can continue onto the next page of an article. Half speed to four times, applied when the voice is generated so it never sounds like a chipmunk.

PDFs and email too

PDFs open in a built-in reader that recovers the text in the right order, even in two-column papers. Gmail and Outlook are read as messages rather than as application clutter, with quoted replies skipped.

Built to be usable

Full keyboard control, adjustable size, spacing and typeface, and highlight colours you can change. The spoken word is underlined as well as coloured, so the cue never depends on colour vision.

Private because of how it is built, not because we promise

There is no server to send anything to. No account, no analytics, no tracking. Your reading history stays in your browser and you can delete it at any time.

Two things do touch the network, and both are described plainly in the privacy policy: voices your browser marks “needs a connection”, which are processed by whoever provides them, and the one-time voice model download.
